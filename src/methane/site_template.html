Effect of the checks

Well-density controls remove the box-wide flare enrichment. Restricting controls to QA-passing pixels makes the uniform day intervals include one too. In the grid analysis, the flaring coefficient drops from 0.513 to 0.030 in 2024 and from 0.621 to 0.109 in 2026 after modelling spatial error.

These tests do not measure methane emitted by flares.

03 / Interpretation

What the analysis can support

The uniform control tests proximity against the whole study box. The well-density control tests it against mapped oil-field locations. Neither comparison identifies the equipment responsible for an individual candidate.

A dated state well inventory, flare activity during each season, and independent same-day plume observations would strengthen the source test.
